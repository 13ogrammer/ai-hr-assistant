# Parental Leave Policy

**Effective Date:** 1 July 2015  
**Last Updated:** 1 November 2026  
**Policy Review Date:** 1 November 2027  
**Document Reference:** HR-POL-004

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy outlines Pinnacle Solutions Pty Ltd's approach to parental leave, exceeding minimum legislative requirements. The company is committed to supporting employees during major life events including childbirth, adoption, and family formation.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time, part-time, and fixed-term employees of Pinnacle Solutions Pty Ltd.

---

## 3. Parental Leave Entitlements

### 3.1 Maternity Leave (Pregnancy and Childbirth)

**Eligibility:** 
- Employee must have completed 12 months continuous service
- Covered by National Employment Standards (Fair Work Act 2009)

**Entitlement:**
- **12 weeks paid maternity leave** (exceeds 6-week minimum requirement)
- Plus up to 12 additional weeks unpaid parental leave
- Total potential leave: 24 weeks from birth

**Access:**
- Taken before and after birth
- Typical pattern: 4-6 weeks before, 6-8 weeks after
- Flexible timing by agreement with manager

### 3.2 Paternity/Partner Leave

**Eligibility:**
- Partner of person giving birth
- 12 months continuous service
- Partner may be same-sex or de facto

**Entitlement:**
- **2 weeks paid paternity leave** (exceeds 1-week minimum)
- Plus up to 12 additional weeks unpaid parental leave
- Typically taken immediately after birth
- May be taken within first 12 months of birth

### 3.3 Adoption Leave

**Eligibility:**
- Employee adopting a child (any age)
- 12 months continuous service at time of placement
- Both partners may access leave

**Entitlement:**
- **3 weeks paid adoption leave** (new parent provision)
- Plus up to 12 additional weeks unpaid parental leave
- Must be taken within 12 months of placement
- Timing flexible by agreement

### 3.4 Parental Leave (Shared)

**For Eligible Employees:**
- Up to 12 weeks total paid leave (shared between parents)
- Plus up to 12 additional weeks unpaid
- Available to primary or secondary carer
- Timing negotiable between parents and company

## 4. Parental Leave Process

### 4.1 Notification

**Pregnancy/Expected Birth:**
1. Notify HR department or manager as soon as pregnancy confirmed
2. Provide expected due date
3. No specific form required, verbal notification accepted

**Notice Periods:**
- Minimum **10 weeks notice** before intended leave start date
- If leave needs to start earlier, provide as much notice as possible
- Notice in writing (email acceptable)

**Adoption/Placement:**
- Notify HR within **7 days of notification** of placement
- Provide placement date or expected placement date
- Documentation of adoption process if requested (for payroll purposes)

### 4.2 Leave Plans and Flexibility

**Initial Plan:**
- Employee and manager discuss intended leave dates and pattern
- Plan should indicate:
  - Start date
  - Duration (paid and unpaid portions)
  - Any intention to return part-time or flexible
  - Communication during leave
  - Return-to-work timing

**Flexibility:**
- Leave plans can be adjusted with reasonable notice
- Changes to timing accommodated where operationally feasible
- Unexpected early birth/placement handled on case-by-case basis

### 4.3 Medical Certification

**Maternity:**
- Employer may require medical certificate to confirm:
  - Expected date of birth
  - Fitness for work status
  - Expected return date

**Certification Requirements:**
- From registered medical practitioner
- No requirement to disclose diagnosis or complications
- Employee has right to discuss health concerns confidentially

---

## 5. Payment During Parental Leave

### 5.1 Paid Leave Payment

Paid parental leave is paid at **ordinary rate of pay**:

- Base salary
- Ordinary allowances
- Regular loadings
- Does NOT include overtime, bonuses, gratuities

### 5.2 Payment Arrangements

**Paid Portion:**
- Paid via normal payroll fortnightly or monthly
- Payment in advance or arrears (employee choice)
- Superannuation continues to be paid by company

**Unpaid Portion:**
- Employee receives no payment
- Superannuation contributions are employee's responsibility (voluntary)
- Can be paid via pre-tax contributions

### 5.3 Annual Leave During Parental Leave

- Annual leave may be taken concurrently with parental leave if desired
- Must be mutually agreed
- Extends total leave period available
- Useful for bridging when returning to work part-time

---

## 6. Superannuation During Parental Leave

### 6.1 Paid Leave Period

- Company continues ordinary superannuation contributions (11.5%)
- Based on ordinary rate of pay
- Contributions treated as employer contributions

### 6.2 Unpaid Leave Period

- Company does NOT contribute during unpaid period
- Employee may elect to make voluntary contributions
- Contributions are concessional (tax-deductible up to limit)
- HR assists with paperwork

---

## 7. Job Security and Return to Work

### 7.1 Job Protection

- Employee's position is protected during parental leave
- Return guaranteed to same position or equivalent
- No adverse action for taking parental leave
- Seniority and accrual of benefits continues

### 7.2 Return-to-Work Discussions

**Prior to Return:**
- HR contacts employee 4 weeks before return date
- Discusses:
  - Return date confirmation
  - Flexible work arrangements
  - Any gradual return-to-work options
  - Childcare arrangement needs
  - Any concerns or support needed

**Return Options:**
1. **Full-time return:** To original position or equivalent
2. **Part-time return:** If feasible and agreed
3. **Gradual return:** Increased hours over weeks/months (if operational)
4. **Different role:** If requested and available

### 7.3 Flexible Work Arrangements

Upon return, employees may request:

- Part-time work arrangement
- Flexible hours (e.g., 7am-3pm, condensed week)
- Work-from-home or hybrid working
- Job sharing (if available)

**Process:**
- Formal request made to HR
- Manager and company consider request
- Meeting held to discuss and agree arrangement
- Can be reviewed after 12 months

---

## 8. Communication During Leave

### 8.1 Company Communication

- Regular company updates sent (optional)
- Employee can opt in/out of communications
- Copies of important announcements offered
- No expectation to respond

### 8.2 Employee Contact

- Employee may contact manager/HR with questions
- Encourage minimal work-related contact
- Emergency contact details provided
- Flexible hours for critical matters (e.g., updating payroll info)

---

## 9. Parental Leave Upon Termination

### 9.1 During Paid Leave

If employment terminates during paid parental leave:

- Remaining paid leave is paid out
- Calculated to termination date
- Included in final paycheck
- Entitlements protected by law

### 9.2 During Unpaid Leave

- Termination during unpaid leave is permitted
- Final pay includes all accrued but untaken paid leave
- Superannuation balances accessible per ATO rules

### 9.3 Redundancy

If employee made redundant:

- Parental leave entitlements treated as normal leave
- Redundancy payment applies
- Minimum statutory protections apply

---

## 10. Carer Leave and Additional Support

### 10.1 Parental Carer Leave (Beyond Parental Leave)

**Eligibility:**
- Employee caring for child/children while on parental leave alternative arrangements

**Entitlement:**
- Up to 5 days per year for carer responsibilities
- Covers childcare emergencies, medical appointments, school matters
- Paid at ordinary rate

### 10.2 Support Services

The company provides:

- **Employee Assistance Program:** Counselling and wellbeing support
- **Flexible working discussions:** Early conversation encouraged
- **Return-to-work coaching:** Easing back into work environment
- **Childcare referral:** Information on childcare options
- **Peer support:** Connection with other parents in company

---

## 11. Discrimination and Protection

### 11.1 Protection from Adverse Action

- Employee protected from dismissal or adverse action for taking parental leave
- Seeking flexible arrangements not grounds for adverse treatment
- Complaints can be made to Fair Work Commission

### 11.2 Parental Status

- Parental or carer status does not affect:
  - Promotion prospects
  - Performance expectations (adjusted for role change)
  - Pay progression
  - Professional development access

### 11.3 Confidentiality

- Personal health information is confidential
- Shared only with relevant HR/payroll staff
- No disclosure to colleagues without consent

---

## 12. Third-Party Parenting

**Step-Parenting/Guardianship:**
- Employees becoming guardians (other than adoption) should discuss with HR
- Flexible arrangements considered case-by-case
- Carer leave (5 days annually) may apply
