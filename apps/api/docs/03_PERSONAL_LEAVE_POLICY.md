# Personal and Sick Leave Policy

**Effective Date:** 1 July 2015  
**Last Updated:** 1 November 2026  
**Policy Review Date:** 1 November 2027  
**Document Reference:** HR-POL-002

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy outlines the personal/sick leave entitlements, usage, and related procedures for all employees of Pinnacle Solutions Pty Ltd. The policy complies with the National Employment Standards under the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth).

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time, part-time, and fixed-term employees of Pinnacle Solutions Pty Ltd.

---

## 3. Personal/Sick Leave Entitlements

### 3.1 Full-Time Employees

**Minimum Entitlement:** 10 days per annum (80 hours per year, or 3.077 hours per week)

Pinnacle Solutions provides **10 days of personal/sick leave per year**, which exceeds the minimum legislative requirement and reflects company commitment to employee wellbeing.

### 3.2 Part-Time Employees

Personal leave accrues on a pro-rata basis:

**Accrual Rate:** Number of hours worked ÷ ordinary hours per week ÷ 52 weeks × 10 days × ordinary hours per week

### 3.3 Fixed-Term Employees

Fixed-term employees accrue personal leave pro-rata from commencement to end of contract.

---

## 4. Use of Personal Leave

### 4.1 Permitted Purposes

Personal/sick leave can be used for:

1. **Personal Illness or Injury**
   - When the employee is unfit to work due to illness, injury, or medical treatment
   - Includes recovery from surgery or medical procedures
   - Mental health conditions and counselling

2. **Carer Responsibilities**
   - Caring for an ill family member or dependant
   - Providing care due to emergency or medical treatment
   - The person being cared for must be a member of the employee's immediate family or household

3. **Appointments**
   - Medical, dental, optical, or psychological appointments
   - Pre-natal and post-natal medical appointments
   - Child immunization appointments
   - Employee may request flexible arrangements as alternative where possible

4. **Bereavement Leave** (see Section 5)
   - While formally separate, bereavement leave is supported under this policy

---

## 5. Bereavement Leave (Compassionate Leave)

### 5.1 Entitlement

Employees are entitled to a reasonable amount of paid bereavement leave upon the death of:

- A member of the employee's immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling)
- A member of the employee's household
- A close relative or person with a significant personal relationship to the employee

### 5.2 Duration

**Typical Allowance:** 3-5 days (paid)

- Duration depends on individual circumstances, relationship to deceased, and requirement to arrange affairs
- Manager will discuss with employee and determine appropriate leave length
- Additional time may be unpaid at manager discretion

### 5.3 Notification and Support

- Employee should notify their manager as soon as practicable
- HR will offer support services including Employee Assistance Program
- Flexible arrangements may be made in the period following bereavement

---

## 6. Leave Request Procedures

### 6.1 Notification Requirements

**For Planned Absences (Medical/Appointments):**
- Provide at least **48 hours notice** where practicable
- Notify manager via phone, email, or HR system
- Specify reason (general category) and duration

**For Unplanned Absences (Illness):**
- Notify manager **before or at the start of the working day** if possible
- If unable to notify in time, contact manager as soon as practicable
- Provide reasonable information about expected duration
- Subsequent days: notify by 9:00 AM each day

**Contact Method:**
- Direct manager (primary contact)
- HR department if manager unavailable
- Employee assistance program if unable to communicate

### 6.2 Medical Certificates

**When Required:**
- Absence of 3 or more consecutive working days
- Pattern of absences (as determined by manager)
- When requested by manager for legitimate operational reasons
- At first instance if employee has taken more than 5 days in a 12-month period

**Certificate Requirements:**
- Medical certificate from registered medical practitioner
- Must contain: date(s) of treatment, statement of fitness for work
- Does NOT need to include medical diagnosis or details
- Copy retained by HR department

**Unreasonable Requests:** 
Management will not request medical certificates for:
- Single days of illness
- First instance of illness in 12-month period (unless pattern emerging)
- Clearly foreseeable medical appointments

### 6.3 Return to Work

Upon return from personal leave:
- Employee completes brief return-to-work check-in with manager
- Reasonable adjustments discussed if required (e.g., flexible hours for recovery)
- Confidentiality is maintained

---

## 7. Accrual and Carryover

### 7.1 Leave Accrual

- Personal leave accrues from the first day of employment
- Accrual occurs continuously throughout the year (typically monthly)
- Accrual continues even if employee is on other forms of leave

### 7.2 Carryover Provisions

**Unlimited Carryover:** 
- Personal leave is not subject to a carryover cap
- Accrued but unused personal leave carries forward indefinitely
- However, employees should use accrued leave regularly to support wellbeing

**Company Expectations:**
- Employees are encouraged to use at least 50% of annual personal leave entitlement each year
- Excessive accrual (beyond 30 days) may trigger manager discussion
- Manager may direct use of leave in consultation with employee if significantly exceeding normal usage

---

## 8. Payment

### 8.1 Rate of Pay

Personal leave is paid at the employee's **ordinary rate of pay**:

- Base salary
- Ordinary allowances
- Regular loadings and penalty rates
- Does NOT include overtime, bonuses, or gratuities

### 8.2 Payment Timing

- Paid at normal payroll processing time
- No deduction from final pay for accrued personal leave upon termination
- Cashed out when employee leaves (if not taken)

---

## 9. Disputes and Medical Clarification

### 9.1 Fitness for Work Disputes

If manager believes employee is fit to work despite claiming illness:

1. Manager may request medical clearance from registered doctor
2. Company may require independent medical assessment (at company cost)
3. Employee has right to be accompanied by support person
4. Decision made on balance of evidence
5. If no agreement, matter referred to HR Manager for mediation

### 9.2 Privacy and Confidentiality

- Medical information is treated as confidential
- Only relevant HR personnel and manager have access
- No information is disclosed without consent (except legally required)
- Medical records stored securely and separately

---

## 10. Patterns of Leave Use

### 10.1 Monitoring

HR monitors patterns of personal leave use to:

- Identify underlying health or wellbeing concerns
- Provide early intervention and support
- Ensure no systematic abuse
- Identify legitimate workplace issues affecting attendance

### 10.2 Intervention

If concerning patterns emerge:

1. Manager initiates supportive discussion with employee
2. Employee is offered access to Employee Assistance Program
3. Workplace adjustments or flexible arrangements discussed
4. If inappropriate use suspected, formal attendance management may be initiated
5. Disciplinary action only after clear process followed

---

## 11. Remote Work During Illness

### 11.1 Partial Capacity Days

If employee feels well enough to work from home but not attend office:

- Manager may approve work-from-home as alternative to leave
- Must be genuine capacity for productive work (not token presence)
- Should not extend illness recovery period
- Employee paid full rate for hours worked

### 11.2 Conditions

- Medical professional confirms capacity for home work
- Work quality not compromised
- No occupational health risk
- Must be negotiated with manager (not automatic)

---

## 12. Mental Health Days

The company recognizes the importance of mental health:

- Personal leave includes mental health conditions
- Mental health days are treated with same respect as physical illness
- Counselling and psychology appointments are permitted
- Employees can access Employee Assistance Program (free, confidential)
- Managers will support mental health needs without stigma

---

## 13. Family and Domestic Violence Leave

### 13.1 Entitlement

The Fair Work Act provides employees experiencing family and domestic violence with:

- **5 days per year** of paid leave
- Additional unpaid leave negotiable with manager
- Flexibility in when leave is taken
- Job security protections

### 13.2 Support Available

- Referral to external support organizations
- Flexible work arrangements
- Confidential discussion with HR
- Workplace safety measures discussed

---

## 14. Termination

### 14.1 Final Payment

Upon employment termination:

- All accrued but untaken personal leave is paid out
- Calculated at ordinary rate of pay
- Included in final paycheck
- No loss of entitlements
