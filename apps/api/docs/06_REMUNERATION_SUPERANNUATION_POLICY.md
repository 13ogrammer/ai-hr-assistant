**Effective Date:** 1 July 2015  
**Last Updated:** 1 November 2026

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the remuneration framework, superannuation arrangements, and compensation structure for all employees of Pinnacle Solutions Pty Ltd. The policy ensures competitive, compliant, and equitable pay practices.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time, part-time, and fixed-term employees. Casual employees are covered under separate casual employment conditions.

---

## 3. Salary and Remuneration Framework

### 3.1 Salary Bands

The company operates a structured salary framework:

| Classification Level | Annual Salary Range | Typical Role Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Entry Level (L1) | $55,000 - $65,000 | Graduate, Junior Consultant |
| Professional (L2) | $70,000 - $90,000 | Consultant, Analyst |
| Senior Professional (L3) | $95,000 - $130,000 | Senior Consultant, Project Lead |
| Manager (L4) | $130,000 - $170,000 | Manager, Team Lead |
| Senior Manager (L5) | $170,000 - $240,000 | Senior Manager, Director |

### 3.2 Salary Determination

Individual salary within bands determined by:

- **Qualifications:** Tertiary degree, professional credentials, specializations
- **Experience:** Relevant industry experience, tenure in role
- **Performance:** Demonstrated capability, track record
- **Market rates:** Industry benchmarks and competitive positioning
- **Role requirements:** Complexity, responsibility, impact
- **Geographic location:** Melbourne CBD, regional adjustments (if applicable)

### 3.3 Salary Setting Process

**For New Hires:**
1. Role classified to appropriate band
2. Market research conducted (salaries.com.au, PayCheck, industry surveys)
3. Offer made within band range based on experience/qualification
4. Approval by HR Manager and Department Head

**Progression:**
- Annual salary review (June each year)
- Merit increases (0-4% typically) based on performance
- Promotion increases may exceed merit range

### 3.4 Pay Equity and Discrimination

The company is committed to:

- **Equal pay for equal work:** Same rate for same role regardless of gender, age, background
- **Pay transparency:** Salary bands published internally
- **Regular audits:** Annual pay equity analysis by gender, age, tenure
- **Complaints:** Fair process for pay disputes

## 4. Remuneration Components

### 4.1 Base Salary

- Ordinary time earnings paid fortnightly
- Fixed amount for full-time employees
- Pro-rata for part-time
- Paid via direct bank transfer
- Superannuation calculated on base salary

### 4.2 Allowances

**Examples of allowances:**

| Allowance Type | Amount | Applicability |
|---|---|---|
| Client Allowance | Varies | Employees with specific client account responsibility |
| Technology Allowance | $500-$1,000 p.a. | Remote/mobile workers for equipment/internet |
| Professional Development | Varies | Professional body memberships (accountants, engineers) |
| Remote Setup | One-off $200-$500 | New remote workers |
| Meals Allowance | Case-by-case | Field/remote work requiring meal arrangements |

**Ordinary Allowances:**
- Part of ordinary rate for leave/superannuation purposes
- Included in redundancy calculations
- Continue during leave if ordinary

**Non-Ordinary Allowances:**
- Not included in leave pay or superannuation (except as specified)
- Ad hoc or discretionary allowances
- Examples: meal reimbursement, travel reimbursement

### 4.3 Bonuses and Performance Pay

**Performance Bonus:**
- Discretionary annual bonus based on company and individual performance
- Typically 0-15% of base salary for applicable roles
- Determined July each year for FY performance
- Paid September/October

**Criteria:**
- Company financial performance
- Individual performance rating (vs objectives)
- Achievement of KPIs
- Attendance and conduct

**Eligibility:**
- Minimum 12 months service by end of financial year
- On-going permanent employment
- Casual and contract workers generally excluded

## 5. Superannuation

### 5.1 Superannuation Contributions

**Statutory Minimum:**
- Employer contribution: 11.5% of ordinary time earnings (2026 rate)
- Requirement under Superannuation Guarantee (Administration) Act 1992 (Cth)
- Rate increases automatically when legislative requirement increases

**Company Fund:**
- Industry Super - Growth Fund (Multi-Asset)
- RSA provider: Industry Super
- Defined contribution structure

### 5.2 Superannuation Elections

**Default Fund:**
- Automatic enrollment to Industry Super
- Employee may nominate alternative fund
- Change of fund available once per 12 months

**Fund Options:**
- Growth (default) - higher growth, higher volatility
- Balanced - medium growth, medium risk
- Conservative - lower growth, lower volatility
- Ethical - ESG-focused investing
- Other ATO-approved funds available

### 5.3 Salary Sacrifice (Concessional Contributions)

Employees may elect to sacrifice portion of pre-tax salary to super:

**Limits (2026):**
- Concessional contribution cap: $27,500 per financial year
- Includes company contribution (11.5%)
- Remaining capacity available for voluntary contributions
- Example: If company contributes $12,000, employee can contribute up to $15,500

**Tax Benefits:**
- Contributions taxed at 15% (plus levy) vs personal marginal rate
- Substantial tax saving for higher income earners
- Accumulation phase earnings also taxed at 15%

**Process:**
- Complete salary sacrifice form with HR
- Nominations effective from next pay cycle
- Annual review recommended
- Can cease at any time

### 5.4 Non-Concessional Contributions

Employees can contribute after-tax dollars:

**Limits (2026):**
- Annual cap: $110,000 per financial year
- Carry-forward mechanism available
- No tax deduction on contribution
- Growth is tax-free once in super

**Co-contribution (Low-Income Earner):**
- Government co-contributes for low-income earners
- Income threshold: less than $42,016 (2026)
- Maximum co-contribution: $500 per year

### 5.5 Superannuation During Leave

**Paid Leave (Annual, Personal, Long Service, Parental):**
- Company contributes employer super on leave pay
- Calculation based on ordinary rate
- Contribution credited to employee's super account

**Unpaid Leave:**
- Company does NOT contribute during unpaid periods
- Employee may elect to make voluntary contributions
- Contributions made via pre-tax salary sacrifice possible

## 6. Payroll Administration

### 6.1 Pay Cycle

**Frequency:** Fortnightly (26 pay periods per year)

**Pay Dates:** Friday each fortnight (with slight variation for public holidays)

**Timing:** Paid via direct bank transfer before 5:00 PM on payday

### 6.2 Pay Slip

Employees receive payslip (digital) each pay period showing:

- Gross salary
- Allowances (itemized)
- Tax withheld (PAYG)
- Superannuation contributions
- Net pay
- Year-to-date totals
- Deductions (if any)

**Access:** Via employee portal or email

### 6.3 Tax Withholding (PAYG)

- Tax withheld based on Tax File Number (TFN) declaration
- Calculated according to ATO tax tables
- Medicare levy withheld (2% normally)
- Annual reconciliation via tax return

**TFN Declaration:**
- Provided at commencement
- Not providing TFN results in highest tax withholding rate (47%)
- Updates can be made anytime

### 6.4 Payroll Deductions

**Permitted Deductions:**
- Income tax (PAYG withholding)
- Superannuation contributions
- Employee salary sacrifice (pre-tax benefits)
- Court-ordered deductions (child support, garnishees)
- Employee-authorized deductions (union fees, charity donations)

**Prohibited Deductions:**
- Deductions that would reduce pay below minimum wage
- Fines or penalties for poor conduct
- Cash shortfalls (not employee's fault)
- Uniform cost (normally)

## 7. Redundancy and Termination Pay

### 7.1 Redundancy Payments

**Eligibility:**
- Genuine redundancy (role abolished, not replaced)
- Minimum service: 12 months
- Protected by Fair Work Act

**Entitlement:**
- Minimum statutory amount per Fair Work Act
- Pinnacle Solutions pays statutory minimum plus:
  - Additional week per year of service (cap 26 weeks maximum)
  - Example: 5 years service = 5 weeks minimum (FW Act) + 5 weeks additional = 10 weeks

**Calculation:**
- Based on ordinary rate of pay at termination
- Includes accrued annual leave, long service leave, personal leave
- Bonus (if applicable) may be included if earned before redundancy announced

### 7.2 Payment Upon Termination

**Standard Termination (Resignation/Dismissal):**

Final payment includes:
- Accrued annual leave (paid out in full)
- Accrued personal leave (paid out in full)
- Accrued long service leave (if applicable)
- Pro-rata annual leave for period worked in final year
- Payment for unused leave loading (if applicable)
- Any superannuation contributions owed

**Timing:** Final payment made within 7 days of termination

### 7.3 Disputed Wages

If employee disputes wage/payment calculations:

1. Employee notifies Payroll Officer within 30 days
2. Payroll conducts calculation audit
3. If error found, payment made within 5 business days
4. If disputed, matter escalated to HR Manager
5. If unresolved, Fair Work claim available

## 8. Expense Reimbursement

### 8.1 Eligible Expenses

**Reimbursable Expenses:**
- Work-related travel (actual cost)
- Meals during work travel (receipts required)
- Client entertainment (within policy limits)
- Professional development/conferences
- Tools and equipment (work-related)
- Internet/phone for work purposes (if remote worker)

### 8.2 Reimbursement Process

1. Employee incurs expense for business purpose
2. Submits receipt/invoice to manager within 30 days
3. Manager approves
4. Payroll processes reimbursement
5. Paid within next payroll cycle (not via salary sacrifice)

### 8.3 Large or Ongoing Expenses

For ongoing work expenses (e.g., internet for remote work):

- Allowance agreed in writing
- Added to pay or reimbursed monthly
- Treated as allowance for tax purposes
- Requires managerial approval

## 9. Professional Development

### 9.1 Training Budget

Each employee receives annual professional development allocation:

**Amount:** $1,500 - $3,000 per annum (varies by level)

**Eligible Uses:**
- External courses and training
- Conferences and seminars
- Professional association memberships
- Tertiary education (partial, if work-related)
- Online learning platforms
- Skills development programs

**Non-Eligible:**
- Personal hobbies or interests
- Non-work-related study
- Entertainment or social events

### 9.2 Study Assistance

**Tertiary Study (Work-Related):**
- Company may contribute to fees/costs
- Typically 50-75% contribution for first degree
- Less for post-graduate study
- Employee time allowance may be provided for study days
- Subject to performance agreement

**Professional Credentials:**
- Accountancy, Law, Engineering, IT credentials supported
- Company may cover examination fees
- Study leave considered (limited)
- Ongoing professional development encouraged

## 10. Remote/Mobile Worker Allowances

### 10.1 Equipment Allowance

Full-time remote workers receive:

**Option 1 - Equipment Provided:**
- Laptop, monitor, keyboard provided by company
- Internet connection reimbursed
- Repairs/support via IT department

**Option 2 - Allowance:**
- $1,500 once-off setup allowance
- Employee responsible for equipment purchase
- Company provides technical support remotely
- No ongoing allowance unless ongoing tech costs

### 10.2 Internet/Phone

- Remote workers working 3+ days from home: Internet reimbursed
- Reimbursement: $50-$100 per month (actual cost)
- Mobile phone allowance: $20-$50 per month (if business use)

## 11. Disputes and Wage Compliance

### 11.1 Wage Disputes

If employee believes wages underpaid:

1. Raise with manager or HR within 30 days
2. HR investigates and audits payroll
3. If underpayment found, back pay calculated
4. Back pay paid within 5 business days
5. If disputed, Fair Work claim available

### 11.2 Minimum Wage Compliance

- Company ensures all employees paid at least National Minimum Wage
- Current (2026): $24.10 per hour (or applicable award rate)
- Audited annually
- Proactive monitoring for compliance

---

## 12. Annual Salary Review

### 12.1 Timing

- Conducted annually in June
- Effective from 1 July (new financial year)
- All employees eligible (after 12+ months service)

### 12.2 Review Process

1. Manager completes performance assessment
2. Employee's salary band confirmed
3. Comparison with market rates
4. Merit increase recommended (typically 2-3%)
5. Review outcome communicated to employee
6. New salary effective 1 July

### 12.3 Merit Assessment

Merit increases considered based on:

- Performance rating (vs objectives)
- Quality of work and contributions
- Reliability and attendance
- Development and learning
- Team collaboration
- Conduct and professionalism

**Typical Ranges:**
- Exceeds expectations: 3-4% increase
- Meets expectations: 2-3% increase
- Developing/below expectation: 0-1% or nil increase
