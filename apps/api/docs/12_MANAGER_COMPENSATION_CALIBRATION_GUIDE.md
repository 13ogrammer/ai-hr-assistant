# Manager Guide to Compensation Calibration

**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL - MANAGER AND HR ONLY
**Effective Date:** 1 July 2025
**Last Updated:** 1 November 2026
**Document Reference:** HR-POL-012

---

## 1. Purpose

This guide helps people managers run the annual compensation calibration round described in the Remuneration and Superannuation Policy. It covers department budget pools, calibration mechanics, and guidance not intended for general staff circulation, since it includes unreleased budget figures.

---

## 2. Department Salary Increase Pools (FY2027)

| Consulting & Advisory | 80 | 3.2% | $410,000 |

| Operations | 10 | 2.5% | $34,000 |

These pools are set by Finance before calibration begins and are not to be shared with direct reports. Employees should only ever be told the outcome for their own role, never the department-wide pool.

---

## 3. Calibration Mechanics

1. Each manager ranks direct reports by performance rating within their team.
2. Proposed increases are entered into the calibration worksheet and must sum to no more than the department's pool.
3. Calibration committee (department head + HR Manager) reviews distributions across departments for consistency.
4. Final increases are locked by 15 June and communicated to employees individually, effective 1 July.

---

## 4. Guidance for Difficult Conversations

- Never disclose another employee's rating, increase, or salary, even in aggregate ranges, to a direct report.
- If an employee asks how their increase compares to a peer, redirect to their own performance and the published salary bands in the Remuneration and Superannuation Policy - do not confirm or deny peer figures.
- Escalate any pressure to disclose pool figures or peer salaries to the HR Manager.
