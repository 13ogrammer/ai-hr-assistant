# Employment Agreement - Chief Executive Officer

**Classification:** RESTRICTED - HR AND BOARD ONLY
**Document Reference:** HR-POL-011
**Parties:** Pinnacle Solutions Pty Ltd ("the Company") and Michael Hartley ("the Executive")

---

## 1. Position and Commencement

The Executive is engaged as Chief Executive Officer of the Company, reporting to the Board of Directors, effective 1 July 2026. The Executive will devote their full working time and attention to the business of the Company and will not, without prior written consent of the Board, engage in any other paid employment or directorship.

---

## 2. Remuneration

The Company will pay the Executive a base salary of $420,000 per annum, inclusive of statutory superannuation, paid fortnightly in accordance with the Company's standard payroll cycle. The Executive is eligible for an annual performance bonus with a target of 30% of base salary, subject to achievement of objectives set by the Board Remuneration Committee each financial year. The Executive will also be granted 50,000 performance rights under the Company's Long-Term Incentive Plan, vesting over three years subject to performance hurdles determined by the Board.

Remuneration under this clause is reviewed annually by the Board Remuneration Committee each May, with any adjustment effective from the following 1 July.

---

## 3. Benefits

The Executive is entitled to participate in the Company's executive benefits program, including a fully maintained motor vehicle or cash equivalent, private health insurance for the Executive and immediate family, and an annual executive health assessment.

---

## 4. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement by giving twelve (12) months' written notice, or payment in lieu thereof calculated on the Executive's base salary at the date of termination. The Company may terminate this agreement without notice in the event of serious misconduct. On termination without cause, the Executive is entitled to a termination payment equivalent to twelve (12) months' base salary and pro-rata performance bonus, in addition to any statutory entitlements.

---

## 5. Restraint of Trade

For a period of twelve (12) months following termination of employment, the Executive must not, without the Board's written consent, provide services to a competing professional services or IT consulting business operating within Victoria, nor solicit the Company's clients or employees.

---

## 6. Confidentiality

The Executive must not, during or after the term of this agreement, disclose any confidential information of the Company, including but not limited to this agreement's terms, client information, financial information, and strategic plans, except as required by law or with the Board's prior written consent.

---

## 7. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of Victoria, Australia, and is subject to the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) to the extent applicable to the Executive's employment.

---

*This agreement and its terms, including remuneration, are confidential between the Executive, the Board, and the HR Manager. Disclosure to any other employee or manager is a serious breach of the Code of Conduct.*

*Document Control: Access restricted to HR and Board roles only. Do not distribute.*
