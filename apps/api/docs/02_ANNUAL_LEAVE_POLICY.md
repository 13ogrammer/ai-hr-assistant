# Annual Leave Policy

**Effective Date:** 1 July 2015  
**Last Updated:** 1 November 2026  
**Policy Review Date:** 1 November 2027  
**Document Reference:** HR-POL-001

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the framework for annual leave entitlements, accrual, request procedures, and payment arrangements for all employees of Pinnacle Solutions Pty Ltd. The policy complies with the National Employment Standards under the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) and the Professional Employees' Award 2020.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time, part-time, and fixed-term employees of Pinnacle Solutions Pty Ltd, regardless of location or classification level.

**Exclusions:** Casual employees are entitled to annual leave loading and other benefits as per the Fair Work Act.

---

## 3. Annual Leave Entitlements

### 3.1 Full-Time Employees

**Minimum Entitlement:** 4 weeks per annum (20 working days or 160 hours)

Pinnacle Solutions provides **4 weeks annual leave per year** as a minimum, in line with National Employment Standards.

**Accrual Method:** Annual leave accrues proportionally from the first day of employment. Employees accrue 3.077 hours per week (based on 38-hour week).

### 3.2 Part-Time Employees

Annual leave accrues on a pro-rata basis according to hours worked:

**Accrual Rate:** Number of weeks worked ÷ 52 weeks × 4 weeks × ordinary hours per week

### 3.3 Fixed-Term Employees

Fixed-term employees are entitled to annual leave on a pro-rata basis calculated from the start date to the end date of their fixed-term contract.

---

## 4. Accrual and Carryover

### 4.1 Leave Accrual

- Annual leave accrues from the employee's commencement date
- Accrual occurs continuously throughout the year
- Accrued leave is credited to the employee's leave balance monthly

### 4.2 Annual Leave Carryover

- Employees may carry over a **maximum of 8 weeks (40 days) of unused annual leave** into the following leave year
- Any leave exceeding 8 weeks must be taken by 31 December of that year or will be forfeited
- The HR Manager may approve exceptions in circumstances where operational requirements prevent leave being taken

### 4.3 Leave Carryover Restrictions (Post-FW Act Changes)

Where an employee has accumulated leave in excess of the legal carryover limits, the following applies:

- The company may direct leave to be taken if it exceeds 8 weeks, provided 28 days notice is given
- Forced leave will be paid at the employee's ordinary rate of pay
- Employees must be consulted on the timing of directed leave

---

## 5. Leave Payment

### 5.1 Rate of Pay

Annual leave is paid at the employee's **ordinary rate of pay**, which includes:

- Base salary
- Allowances (where applicable and ordinary in nature)
- Regular penalty rates, loadings, and commissions
- Does NOT include overtime, performance bonuses, or gratuities

### 5.2 Payment Timing

- Annual leave is paid in advance (prior to taking leave) via normal payroll procedures
- Leave payment is processed at the end of the pay period in which leave is taken
- Payment is made at the employee's regular rate regardless of when the leave is taken

---

## 6. Leave Requesting Procedures

### 6.1 Leave Application Process

1. **Notice Period:** Employees should provide written notice of their intention to take annual leave
   - Standard notice: **4 weeks** for periods of leave exceeding 5 consecutive working days
   - Short notice: **2 weeks** for periods of leave under 5 consecutive working days
   - Emergency leave: Approved by line manager on case-by-case basis

2. **Application Method:**
   - Submit leave request via HR system (if available) OR
   - Submit written request to your direct manager and HR department
   - Include: dates of leave, return date, and any handover requirements

3. **Approval Process:**
   - Line manager reviews and approves/denies within 5 business days
   - HR verifies leave balance and processes approval
   - Employee receives confirmation of approved leave

### 6.2 Leave Request Denial

Leave requests may be denied only where:
- Leave balance is insufficient
- Operational requirements prevent approval (with alternative dates offered)
- Leave would create critical service gaps that cannot be managed

**Notice of Denial:** Must be provided as soon as practicable, with reasons and alternative dates offered.

---

## 7. Taking Annual Leave

### 7.1 Timing and Coordination

- Employees should attempt to take leave in consultation with their manager and team
- Leave timing should support business operations where practicable
- The company may direct timing of leave if necessary to manage operational requirements (with 28 days notice)

### 7.2 Duration

- Employees may take leave in blocks of any length, from individual days to continuous periods
- Leave may be taken in multiple periods throughout the year
- Timing should be coordinated with team requirements

### 7.3 Public Holidays During Leave

If public holidays fall during a period of annual leave:

- Employees are entitled to have public holiday(s) treated as separate entitlement
- The employee may defer leave or receive additional pay for the public holiday
- This will be calculated and paid in accordance with the Fair Work Act

---

## 8. Leave Upon Termination

### 8.1 Final Leave Payment

Upon termination of employment (regardless of reason):

- All accrued but untaken annual leave is paid out at the employee's ordinary rate of pay
- Payment is made in the final paycheck
- No leave lapses or is forfeited upon termination

### 8.2 Notice Period Leave

Leave taken during the notice period is treated as normal annual leave and is paid accordingly.

---

## 9. Additional Leave Provisions

### 9.1 Extended Leave Option

Employees may apply to take unpaid leave with manager approval for:
- Career breaks or sabbaticals
- Family/caring responsibilities
- Personal development
- Subject to operational requirements

### 9.2 Study Leave

Employees undertaking approved tertiary or professional study may request flexible arrangements or short-term leave (with manager discretion).

---

## 10. Disputes and Variations

### 10.1 Leave Balance Disputes

If an employee disputes their leave balance:
1. Employee contacts HR department within 5 business days
2. HR conducts audit of leave records
3. Discrepancy is resolved and corrected if necessary
4. Employee receives written confirmation of correct balance

### 10.2 Variations to This Policy

Any variations to leave arrangements must be:
- Approved in writing by the HR Manager
- At least as favourable to the employee as the Fair Work Act requires
- Documented in the employee file

---

- Changes to legislation are incorporated immediately
- Employees are notified of any changes via email and the employee handbook
