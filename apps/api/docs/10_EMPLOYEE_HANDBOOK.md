## 3. Employment Relationships

### 3.1 Employment Types

**Permanent Full-Time**
- 38 hours per week
- Ongoing employment
- Full benefits
- Entitled to all leave provisions

**Permanent Part-Time**
- Fixed hours per week (less than 38)
- Ongoing employment
- Pro-rata benefits
- Entitled to all leave provisions (pro-rata)

**Fixed-Term Contract**
- Defined contract period
- May be full-time or part-time
- Benefits and leave entitlements apply
- Contract renewal or termination at end date

**Casual**
- Ongoing employment without fixed hours
- Engaged on as-needed basis
- No annual leave entitlement (paid via loading)
- Personal leave and long service leave provisions apply

### 3.2 Your Employment Contract

Your contract of employment outlines:
- Position title and reporting line
- Hours and location
- Salary and benefits
- Notice periods
- Termination conditions
- Any conditions specific to your role

Keep a copy for your records and contact HR if you have questions.

## 7. Leave Entitlements

### 7.1 Annual Leave

**Entitlement:** 4 weeks per year (20 days or 160 hours)

**How it Works:**
- Accrues each pay period
- Carry forward up to 8 weeks (40 days)
- Paid at ordinary rate + any loading
- Taken by mutual agreement

**Requesting Leave:**
- Provide 4 weeks notice for extended leave
- Provide 2 weeks notice for short leave
- Submit via manager or HR
- Approved based on business needs

### 7.2 Personal/Sick Leave

**Entitlement:** 10 days per year (80 hours)

**Uses:**
- Personal illness or injury
- Medical appointments
- Carer responsibilities
- Bereavement leave

**Requesting:**
- Notify manager before or at start of work day (if possible)
- Medical certificate required for 3+ consecutive days
- No pay deduction for legitimate use

### 7.3 Long Service Leave

**Eligibility:** After 10 years continuous service

**Entitlement:** 8.67 weeks per 10 years of service (plus 17.5% loading)

**Taking Leave:**
- By agreement with employer
- Typically in blocks of 1+ weeks
- Approved with reasonable notice
- Paid at ordinary rate + loading

### 7.4 Parental Leave

**Maternity Leave:** 12 weeks paid (exceeds statutory minimum)

**Paternity Leave:** 2 weeks paid

**Adoption Leave:** 3 weeks paid

**Plus:** Up to 12 weeks additional unpaid parental leave

**More Information:** See Parental Leave Policy

### 7.5 Bereavement Leave

**Entitlement:** 3-5 days paid leave upon death of close family member

**Process:**
- Notify manager immediately
- Discuss appropriate leave duration
- Support offered via EAP

## 8. Health and Safety

### 8.1 Our Commitment

We're committed to providing a safe workplace. Everyone is responsible for safety.

**Our Commitment:**
- Identify and eliminate workplace hazards
- Provide safe equipment and systems
- Train employees on safety procedures
- Investigate incidents promptly
- Support injured workers' recovery
- Maintain safe environment

**Your Responsibility:**
- Follow safety procedures
- Report hazards immediately
- Use safety equipment provided
- Don't work if unfit or impaired
- Look out for colleagues' safety

### 8.2 Hazard Reporting

**If you identify a hazard:**
1. Tell your manager immediately
2. Complete hazard report form
3. HR will assess and act on hazard
4. Updates provided on actions taken

**Protected:** You cannot be penalized for good-faith hazard reporting.

### 8.3 Incident Reporting

**If an incident or injury occurs:**
1. Notify manager immediately
2. Receive first aid if needed
3. Report details to HR within 24 hours
4. HR will investigate and follow up
5. Workers' compensation claim filed (if applicable)

### 8.4 Emergency Procedures

**Fire Evacuation:**
- Calmly exit building using nearest exit
- Proceed to assembly point (outside building)
- Wait for all-clear before re-entering
- Fire drills held annually

**Medical Emergency:**
- Notify manager/first-aider immediately
- Call 000 if serious
- First aid administered
- Incident reported to HR

**Severe Weather:**
- Manager advises if evacuation needed
- Follow emergency procedures
- Updates provided as situation develops

### 8.5 Wellbeing Support

**Employee Assistance Program:**
- Free, confidential counselling
- Available 24/7 for crisis support
- Covers personal, family, or work issues
- Contact: 1300 783 556

**Mental Health Support:**
- Mental health days treated same as physical illness
- Flexible arrangements for mental health treatment
- No stigma for mental health concerns

## 11. Dispute Resolution

### 11.1 Addressing Concerns

**Step 1: Direct Discussion**
- Talk to your manager about the concern
- Many issues resolved through discussion
- Open, honest communication

**Step 2: HR Involvement**
- If unresolved, contact HR
- Formal discussion with HR and manager
- Mediation offered if helpful

**Step 3: Formal Process**
- Escalate to HR Manager if needed
- Formal investigation if appropriate
- Written response provided

**Step 4: External Resolution**
- Fair Work Commission
- State tribunal (if discrimination matter)
- External mediation service

**Protection:**
- No retaliation for raising concerns
- Confidentiality maintained
- Support available throughout process

### 11.2 Grievance Procedure

**Submitting a Grievance:**
- Written notice to HR
- Detailed description of concern
- Desired resolution
- Meeting within 5 business days

**Investigation:**
- Facts gathered
- Parties interviewed
- Evidence reviewed
- Fair process followed

**Decision:**
- Written response within 10 business days
- Reasons for decision provided
- Right to appeal

**Appeal:**
- Request review within 5 business days
- HR Manager reviews decision
- Final decision communicated

## 12. Frequently Asked Questions

### 12.1 Leave Questions

**Q: Can I carry over my annual leave?**
A: Yes, up to 8 weeks. Leave beyond 8 weeks must be taken or forfeited.

**Q: Do I get paid for annual leave?**
A: Yes, at your ordinary rate of pay. Long service leave also receives 17.5% loading.

**Q: What if I'm sick and don't have a medical certificate?**
A: Medical certificate required for absences of 3+ consecutive days, or if pattern emerging.

**Q: Can I take unpaid leave?**
A: Unpaid leave can be requested and considered by manager on case-by-case basis.

### 12.2 Work Arrangements

**Q: Can I work from home?**
A: Remote working available for eligible roles. Discuss with your manager.

**Q: Can I change my hours?**
A: Flexible arrangements available. Submit request to manager for consideration.

**Q: What happens if I'm injured at work?**
A: Report immediately. First aid provided, incident investigated, workers' compensation claim filed.

### 12.3 Pay and Superannuation

**Q: When do I get paid?**
A: Fortnightly on Fridays via direct bank transfer.

**Q: How is my superannuation contribution calculated?**
A: 11.5% of ordinary time earnings, paid to your nominated fund.

**Q: Can I choose a different superannuation fund?**
A: Yes, you can nominate an ATO-approved fund. Contact HR.

**Q: Will my pay be reviewed?**
A: Yes, annually in June. Review based on performance and market rates.

### 12.4 Conduct and Performance

**Q: What happens if I make a mistake?**
A: Everyone makes mistakes. We support learning and improvement. Serious or repeated mistakes addressed through performance management.

**Q: How am I assessed on performance?**
A: Based on achievement of objectives, demonstration of competencies, and adherence to company values.

**Q: What if I disagree with my performance rating?**
A: You have the right to dispute. Discuss with manager, escalate to HR if unresolved.

**Q: What is the disciplinary process?**
A: For conduct issues: investigation, opportunity to respond, fair hearing, decision, potential appeal.
