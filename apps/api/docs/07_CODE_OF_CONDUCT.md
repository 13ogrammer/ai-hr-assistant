# Code of Conduct

**Effective Date:** 1 July 2015  
**Last Updated:** 1 November 2026  
**Policy Review Date:** 1 November 2027**  
**Document Reference:** HR-POL-007

---

## 1. Purpose and Principles

This Code of Conduct establishes the standards of professional and ethical behaviour expected of all employees of Pinnacle Solutions Pty Ltd. The code reflects the company's commitment to:

- **Integrity:** Acting honestly and ethically
- **Respect:** Treating everyone with dignity and equality
- **Accountability:** Taking responsibility for actions
- **Professionalism:** Maintaining high standards
- **Compliance:** Following laws and company policies

---

## 2. Scope

This Code applies to all full-time, part-time, fixed-term, and casual employees, regardless of location or role level.

**External Applicability:**
- Directors and senior management held to higher standard
- Contractors and third parties expected to comply while working for company
- Compliance included in contract/engagement terms

---

## 3. General Conduct Expectations

### 3.1 Professional Behaviour

All employees are expected to:

- Conduct themselves professionally at all times
- Treat colleagues, clients, and stakeholders with respect and courtesy
- Avoid conduct that would bring disrepute to the company
- Maintain professional appearance appropriate to role and client interaction
- Communicate professionally in all forms (written, verbal, electronic)

### 3.2 Respectful Workplace

The company maintains a respectful, inclusive workplace where:

- Discrimination, harassment, and bullying are not tolerated
- Diversity is valued and protected
- Confidentiality and privacy are respected
- Personal information is handled appropriately
- Psychological safety exists for all employees

### 3.3 Accountability

Employees are accountable for:

- Quality of work and meeting deadlines
- Acting in company's best interests
- Following policies and procedures
- Escalating concerns appropriately
- Honest communication and reporting

---

## 4. Workplace Conduct Standards

### 4.1 Attendance and Punctuality

- Arrive on time for work and scheduled commitments
- Notify manager if unable to attend work
- Plan leave in advance (except emergencies)
- Minimize absenteeism
- Be reliably available during agreed working hours

### 4.2 Work Diligence

- Perform duties to the best of your ability
- Deliver quality work and meet deadlines
- Follow established processes and procedures
- Ask for help or clarification when needed
- Continuously develop skills and knowledge

### 4.3 Communication and Collaboration

- Communicate clearly and honestly
- Listen actively to colleagues and clients
- Share information necessary for team effectiveness
- Work cooperatively with team members
- Provide constructive feedback
- Escalate issues appropriately rather than complaining

### 4.4 Confidentiality and Privacy

- Maintain confidentiality of company information
- Do not disclose client information
- Respect employee privacy
- Use personal data only for legitimate work purposes
- Comply with privacy law obligations
- Return all company information upon termination

### 4.5 Conflict of Interest

Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict with company interests:

**Examples of Conflicts:**
- Secondary employment competing with company business
- Accepting gifts from clients or suppliers (except token items)
- Transacting business with family members
- Using company information for personal gain
- Financial interest in company suppliers/competitors

**Management of Conflicts:**
- Disclose any potential conflicts to manager/HR
- Recuse yourself from decisions involving conflict
- Seek written approval from HR before proceeding
- Document approval

**Prohibited Conflicts:**
- Nepotism: Employing close family members in authority relationships
- Corruption: Accepting bribes or inducements
- Self-dealing: Using position for personal financial gain

## 5. Discrimination and Harassment Prevention

### 5.1 Zero Tolerance Policy

The company has zero tolerance for:

- **Discrimination:** Treating someone unfairly based on protected attributes
- **Harassment:** Unwelcome conduct that offends, humiliates, or intimidates
- **Bullying:** Repeated unreasonable behavior intended to harm
- **Sexual harassment:** Unwelcome sexual conduct or advances
- **Vilification:** Attacking someone based on protected attributes

### 5.2 Protected Attributes

Discrimination is prohibited based on:

- Gender (including gender identity, pregnancy)
- Age
- Race, ethnicity, national origin
- Disability
- Sexual orientation
- Religion or belief
- Marital or family status
- Carer responsibility
- Impairment
- Political belief or activity
- Paid parental leave status

### 5.3 Reporting Harassment

If experiencing or witnessing harassment:

1. **Tell the person to stop** (if safe to do so)
2. **Report to your manager** (unless manager involved)
3. **Report to HR** (formal process)
4. **External complaint:** Fair Work Commission, AHRC, or Victoria state tribunal
5. **Support:** Employee Assistance Program available (free, confidential)

**No Retaliation:**
- Employees protected from adverse action for making complaint
- Retaliation is prohibited
- Confidentiality maintained as far as possible

### 5.4 Investigation and Resolution

- All complaints investigated promptly and fairly
- Both parties have right to be heard
- Findings documented
- Action taken if breach confirmed
- Support offered to parties during process

## 6. Health and Safety

### 6.1 Commitment to Safety

All employees are responsible for:

- Complying with OHS legislation
- Following safety procedures and guidelines
- Using safety equipment provided
- Reporting hazards and incidents
- Not working under influence of drugs/alcohol
- Supporting mental health and wellbeing of colleagues

### 6.2 Work-Life Balance

The company supports:

- Reasonable working hours (generally 38 hours per week)
- Regular breaks during work day
- Use of annual leave and personal leave
- Time for medical appointments
- Flexible arrangements where feasible
- Assistance for employees in distress

### 6.3 Substance Abuse

- Working under influence of drugs/alcohol is prohibited
- Employees with substance misuse concerns encouraged to seek help
- Support available via Employee Assistance Program
- Disciplinary action taken for impairment at work
- Pre-employment and random drug testing may be conducted for safety-sensitive roles

### 6.4 Mental Health Support

The company recognizes mental health as important:

- Employees encouraged to seek help if struggling
- Confidential counselling available (EAP)
- Stress management and resilience support
- Flexible arrangements for mental health treatment
- No stigma for mental health conditions
- Peer support networks available

## 7. Conflicts and Disputes

### 7.1 Professional Disagreement

- Professional disagreement and debate are healthy
- Express disagreement respectfully
- Focus on issues, not personalities
- Support team decisions once made
- Use appropriate channels to escalate concerns

### 7.2 Disputes with Colleagues

If experiencing conflict with colleague:

1. Attempt to resolve directly and respectfully
2. Involve manager if direct resolution unsuccessful
3. Manager facilitates resolution discussion
4. If unresolved, formal mediation offered
5. As last resort, formal grievance process

### 7.3 Disputes with Management

If conflict with manager:

1. Attempt discussion with manager (if safe/appropriate)
2. Escalate to HR department
3. HR facilitates resolution
4. Formal mediation if necessary
5. External support (e.g., Fair Work ombudsman)

### 7.4 Grievance Process

**Formal Grievance:**
- Employee submits written grievance to HR
- Outlines issue, desired resolution
- Meeting held within 5 business days
- Investigation conducted
- Written response provided within 10 business days
- Right to appeal to HR Manager within 5 days

## 8. Technology and Digital Communication

### 8.1 Responsible Use

Company equipment and systems must be used:

- For work purposes (personal use minimal and reasonable)
- In accordance with security policies
- Without accessing inappropriate content
- With password protection
- Without sharing access with others
- Compliantly with all legislation

### 8.2 Email and Digital Communication

- Professional tone required
- Assume messages may be forwarded or reviewed
- Do not send defamatory, discriminatory, or harassing content
- Respect others' time (avoid excessive cc/reply-all)
- Use secure channels for sensitive information
- Delete unnecessary messages to manage storage

### 8.3 Social Media

- Do not disclose company confidential information
- Do not make defamatory statements about company or colleagues
- Clearly state personal opinions are your own (not company's)
- Be aware posts may identify you as company employee
- Company may monitor public posts mentioning company
- Violation may result in disciplinary action

### 8.4 Privacy and Surveillance

- Company may monitor company equipment and systems
- Personal privacy in use of company systems is limited
- Monitoring for: security, performance, compliance, legal obligation
- Employees notified of monitoring practices
- Personal devices not monitored

---

## 9. External Representations

### 9.1 Media and Public Statements

- Do not make public statements on behalf of company
- Refer media inquiries to management
- Personal statements must not claim to represent company
- Refrain from public criticism of company
- Avoid posts that could damage company reputation

### 9.2 Political and Community Activity

- Employees free to engage in political activity in personal time
- Do not represent company in political activity
- Do not use company resources for political purposes
- Company remains politically neutral
- No retaliation for lawful political activity

---

## 10. Compliance and Legal Obligations

### 10.1 Compliance with Laws

All employees must:

- Obey all applicable laws and regulations
- Report suspected legal breaches
- Cooperate with regulatory investigations
- Not assist in illegal activity
- Seek legal/compliance advice when uncertain

### 10.2 Financial Accountability

- Keep accurate work records
- Record time and expenses honestly
- Do not falsify timesheets or expense claims
- Use company funds responsibly
- Report financial irregularities

### 10.3 Intellectual Property

- Work created for company belongs to company
- Company software, documents, processes are confidential
- Do not remove company property
- Do not use company IP for personal business
- Respect others' intellectual property

### 10.4 Gifts and Benefits

**Acceptable Gifts:**
- Token gifts (under $50 value)
- Company branded merchandise
- Hospitality of minimal value
- Gifts from established business relationships

**Prohibited Gifts:**
- Cash or equivalent
- Expensive personal gifts
- Gifts intended to influence business decision
- Gifts that create conflict of interest
- Gifts conditioned on business outcome

## 11. Breach of Code

### 11.1 Consequences

Breaches of the Code of Conduct may result in:

- **Verbal warning:** First minor breach
- **Written warning:** Continued or more serious breach
- **Final warning:** Serious breach or repeated violations
- **Suspension:** Pending investigation of serious allegations
- **Dismissal:** For serious misconduct, repeated breaches, or loss of trust

### 11.2 Serious Misconduct

Acts constituting serious misconduct:

- Dishonesty or fraud
- Theft or misappropriation
- Physical violence
- Sexual harassment or assault
- Discrimination or vilification
- Breach of confidentiality causing harm
- Working under influence
- Willful destruction of property
- Being convicted of serious criminal offense

**Consequence:** Dismissal without notice

### 11.3 Disciplinary Process

1. **Investigation:** Facts gathered, parties interviewed
2. **Interview:** Employee given opportunity to respond
3. **Decision:** Determination of breach and consequence
4. **Communication:** Written notification of action
5. **Appeal:** Right to appeal to HR Manager within 5 days

---

## 12. Confidentiality Upon Leaving

Upon termination, employees must:

- Return all company property
- Maintain confidentiality of company information
- Not use company IP for competing business
- Not solicit company clients or employees
- Comply with any restraint agreement
- Deactivate all system access
