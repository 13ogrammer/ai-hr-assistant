## 1. Purpose

This register records the actual remuneration of Pinnacle Solutions' executive team for payroll, audit, and board reporting purposes. It is not a policy document and is not to be distributed outside the HR Manager and Payroll & Benefits Officer.

---

## 3. Review Cycle

Executive remuneration is reviewed annually by the Board Remuneration Committee each May, with changes effective 1 July. Any interim adjustment requires Board approval.

---

## 4. Confidentiality

Disclosure of any figure in this register to an employee, manager, or external party outside the Remuneration Committee and HR is a serious breach of the Code of Conduct and may result in disciplinary action.
