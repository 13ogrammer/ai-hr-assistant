# Long Service Leave Policy

**Effective Date:** 1 July 2015  
**Last Updated:** 1 November 2026  
**Policy Review Date:** 1 November 2027  
**Document Reference:** HR-POL-003

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the framework for Long Service Leave (LSL) entitlements, accrual, taking of leave, and payment arrangements for all eligible employees of Pinnacle Solutions Pty Ltd in accordance with the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) and the Professional Employees' Award 2020.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time, part-time, and fixed-term employees who have completed the minimum service requirements.

**Casual employees** are covered under specific casual long service leave provisions (see Section 3.3).

---

## 3. Long Service Leave Entitlements

### 3.1 Full-Time Employees

**Eligibility:** After 10 years of continuous service

**Entitlement:** 8.6667 weeks per 10 years of service (or 13 weeks per 15 years of service)

**Accrual Rate:** 1.615 weeks per year of service (based on 38-hour week)

### 3.2 Part-Time Employees

LSL accrues on a pro-rata basis according to ordinary hours worked:

**Accrual Rate:** (Hours worked per week ÷ Ordinary hours per week) × 1.615 weeks per year

### 3.3 Casual Employees

Casual employees are entitled to LSL after 10 years of continuous service:

**Entitlement:** 8.6667 weeks paid leave at ordinary rate

Casual LSL is calculated and paid out when:
- Employee reaches 10 years service, or
- Employment ends

### 3.4 Fixed-Term Employees

Fixed-term employees accrue LSL on a pro-rata basis:

**Calculation:** Duration of contract (in years) × accrual rate per annum × ordinary hours per week

---

## 4. Accrual and Service Calculation

### 4.1 Continuous Service

For LSL purposes, "continuous service" means:

- **Commences:** First day of employment
- **Includes:** All periods of paid leave, unpaid leave (up to 26 weeks), jury duty, etc.
- **Excludes:** Periods where employment was terminated and then re-commenced (unless rehire agreement)

### 4.2 Service Breaks

Service is NOT broken by:

- Annual leave, personal leave, or long service leave
- Authorized unpaid leave (up to 26 weeks)
- Jury duty or court-required leave
- Military service (with relevant agreements)
- Authorized strike action

Service IS broken by:

- Resignation or dismissal
- Reaching retirement age or death
- Termination for misconduct
- Voluntary redundancy accepted (with accrued LSL payable)

### 4.3 Service Recognition Upon Hire

An employee's service period may include prior service with:

- Related companies/associated entities (if agreement)
- Previous employers (if employed in similar role and no gap > 2 years)
- Subject to documentary evidence and written agreement

---

## 5. Taking Long Service Leave

### 5.1 Access and Timing

After 10 years of continuous service, employees may take LSL:

- **When:** At any time by mutual agreement with employer
- **Duration:** In blocks of any length, or in multiple periods
- **Flexibility:** Subject to operational requirements

### 5.2 Employer-Initiated Leave

After 10 years of service, if employer directs leave:

- **Notice Period:** 12 months notice required
- **Reason:** To manage operational requirements
- **Timing:** Employer and employee to negotiate timing
- **Consultation:** Employee has right to be consulted on dates

### 5.3 Leave Request Process

**Requesting Leave:**

1. Employee submits written request to HR Manager
   - Proposed dates (or flexible dates)
   - Duration of leave required
   - Justification (rest, travel, personal reasons - optional)

2. HR reviews leave balance and operational impact

3. Manager and employee agree timing:
   - Minimum **8 weeks notice** for leave under 4 weeks
   - Minimum **12 weeks notice** for leave 4+ weeks
   - Earlier approval if emergencies/compassionate reasons

4. Written confirmation issued

### 5.4 Splitting Leave

Employees may request to take LSL in multiple periods:

- Examples: 2-3 week breaks spread across 2-3 years
- Must be agreed with employer
- Minimum 1 week per split period preferred
- Each period must be separate arrangement

---

## 6. Payment During Long Service Leave

### 6.1 Rate of Pay

Employees on LSL are paid at their **ordinary rate of pay**:

- Base salary
- Ordinary allowances
- Regular loadings and commissions
- Does NOT include overtime, performance bonuses, gratuities

### 6.2 Leave Loading

The company provides additional compensation:

**Loading:** 17.5% of ordinary rate of pay for LSL period

- Designed to provide additional remuneration for rest
- Applied to full LSL periods taken
- Calculated on ordinary rate of pay

### 6.3 Payment Arrangements

- Payment made via normal payroll during leave period
- Paid in advance where practicable
- All leave pay included in final paycheck upon termination

---

## 7. Long Service Leave Upon Termination

### 7.1 Termination by Employer

Upon termination of employment by employer:

- Accrued but untaken LSL is paid out in full
- Calculated to the date of termination
- Paid at ordinary rate plus 17.5% loading
- Included in final paycheck

### 7.2 Termination by Employee

Upon resignation or voluntary termination:

- Accrued but untaken LSL is paid out in full
- Exception: Termination for serious misconduct may result in forfeiture (rare circumstance)
- Normal terminations: Full payout of all accrued LSL at ordinary rate + loading

### 7.3 Redundancy

Upon redundancy (genuine redundancy as per FW Act):

- All accrued LSL is paid out
- Paid at ordinary rate plus 17.5% loading
- May be combined with redundancy payment

---

## 8. Sick Leave During Long Service Leave

### 8.1 Impact of Illness

If employee becomes ill during approved LSL:

- Employee may substitute genuine sick days with personal leave
- Must provide medical certificate
- Manager approval for substitution
- Balance of approved LSL remains available

### 8.2 Rescheduling

If serious illness/injury prevents taking planned LSL:

- Leave may be rescheduled within reasonable timeframe
- Medical evidence may be required
- Reasonable efforts to reschedule within 6-12 months

---

## 9. Other Leave During or After LSL

### 9.1 Annual Leave and LSL

- Annual leave can be taken before, during, or after LSL
- Separate entitlements managed independently
- Both can be taken in combination if agreed

### 9.2 Public Holidays During LSL

If public holidays fall during LSL:

- Public holiday period not charged to LSL
- Employee receives public holiday pay
- LSL period extends or employee receives additional pay

---

## 10. Disputes and Queries

### 10.1 Service Calculation Disputes

If an employee disputes their service calculation:

1. Employee notifies HR within 10 business days of concern
2. HR audits service records and calculations
3. If discrepancy found, correction made and backdated if necessary
4. Written confirmation provided to employee
5. If still disputed, formal determination process initiated

### 10.2 Leave Balance Verification

Employees may request verification of LSL balance:

- HR provides written statement
- Available via employee portal or HR system
- Updated annually in June

---

## 11. Record Keeping

The company maintains records of:

- Employee service dates and breaks in service
- LSL accrual calculations
- Leave taken dates and amounts
- Payments made
- Records retained for 7 years minimum
