## 1. Purpose and Commitment

Pinnacle Solutions Pty Ltd is committed to providing a safe, healthy, and secure workplace for all employees, contractors, and visitors. This policy establishes the framework for managing occupational health and safety (OHS) in accordance with the Occupational Health and Safety Act 2004 (Vic) and best practice standards.

**Commitment Statement:**
We believe that workplace safety is not negotiable and that all injuries and incidents are preventable through proper management, training, and employee engagement.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All full-time, part-time, and fixed-term employees
- Contractors and temporary staff working at company premises
- Visitors and external stakeholders
- All company locations and work-related activities
- Remote workers (duty of care applies)

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

### 3.1 Executive Management

- Ensure sufficient resources for OHS management
- Set OHS performance metrics and review
- Embed OHS into company culture and strategy
- Approve OHS policies and procedures
- Visible leadership on safety matters
- Accountability for OHS performance

### 3.2 Managers and Supervisors

- Implement OHS policies and procedures in their areas

- Induct new team members on OHS requirements

- Report hazards, incidents, and injuries immediately
- Cooperate with incident investigations
- Not work under influence of drugs/alcohol
- Look out for colleagues' safety

- Elected from employee representatives
- Inspected workplace for hazards

## 4. Hazard Identification and Risk Management

### 4.1 Common Workplace Hazards

**Physical Hazards:**
- Ergonomic issues (poor workstation setup)
- Repetitive strain injury
- Slips, trips, falls
- Manual handling
- Noise exposure

**Psychological Hazards:**
- Workplace stress
- Bullying or harassment
- Work overload
- Inadequate support
- Job insecurity

**Environmental Hazards:**
- Temperature extremes
- Poor lighting or air quality
- Confined spaces
- Electrical hazards
- Chemical exposure (if applicable)

### 4.2 Risk Assessment Process

**Hazard Identification:**
1. Observe work areas and work practices
2. Review incident and injury history
3. Consult with employees
4. Review regulations and standards
5. Document all potential hazards

**Risk Evaluation:**
- Assess likelihood of harm
- Assess severity of potential harm
- Assign risk rating (high/medium/low)
- Prioritize by risk level

**Control Implementation:**
1. **Eliminate:** Remove hazard if possible (first option)
2. **Substitute:** Replace with less hazardous alternative
3. **Engineer:** Modify equipment or environment
4. **Administrative:** Change procedures or practices
5. **PPE:** Provide personal protective equipment (last resort)

**Review and Monitor:**
- Check effectiveness of controls
- Update as work methods change
- Annual review of risk register
- Continuous monitoring

### 4.3 Hazard Reporting

Employees who identify hazards should:

1. Report to their manager immediately
2. If urgent hazard, report to manager and OHS representative
3. Use OHS incident/hazard form (paper or online)
4. Provide detailed information:
   - Location and description of hazard
   - Who might be affected
   - Potential consequences
   - Any near-miss incidents

**Management Response:**
- Hazards reviewed within 2 business days
- Risk assessment conducted
- Controls implemented or timeline established
- Employee informed of action taken
- No penalty for good-faith reporting

## 5. Workplace Incidents and Injuries

### 5.1 Incident Reporting

**Definition:** Incident = unplanned event causing or potentially causing injury, illness, or property damage

**Reporting Requirements:**

**Immediate Actions:**
- Ensure injured person receives first aid if needed
- Remove person from hazardous situation if safe
- Assess immediate danger
- Call emergency services (000) if serious injury

**Incident Report (within 24 hours):**
- Report to manager and HR
- Use incident report form
- Include:
  - Date, time, location
  - Person(s) involved
  - Detailed description of what happened
  - Injuries or damage
  - Witness names and contact details
  - Immediate actions taken

**Investigation (within 5 business days):**
- HR and manager investigate
- Interview involved parties and witnesses
- Inspect site and conditions
- Review work procedures
- Identify root causes
- Recommend preventive actions

**Notification Requirements:**
- WorkSafe Victoria notified if serious injury (within 24 hours)
- Workers' compensation claim filed if applicable
- Regulator notified if required by law

### 5.2 Serious Injury Reporting

**Notifiable Injuries (WorkSafe Victoria):**
- Death
- Serious head, neck, or back injury
- Serious spinal injury
- Serious eye injury
- Amputation
- Serious burn injury
- Loss of consciousness
- Hospitalization overnight

**Serious Injury Process:**
- Immediate notification to WorkSafe (24 hours)
- Preserve incident scene
- Do not move equipment unless necessary
- Cooperative with investigation
- Documentation maintained

## 6. Workers' Compensation

### 6.1 Entitlement

All employees are covered by workers' compensation insurance:

**Coverage Includes:**
- Medical and hospital expenses
- Rehabilitation services
- Wages replacement (up to 75% of ordinary wages, maximum 13 weeks)
- Permanent disability benefits
- Lump sum death benefits
- Return-to-work support

### 6.2 Claim Process

**If Injured at Work:**

1. **Report Immediately:** Tell your manager/supervisor
2. **First Aid:** Receive treatment if needed
3. **Medical Treatment:** Visit doctor (nominated provider or your choice)
4. **Claim Form:** Complete workers' compensation claim form within 30 days
5. **Employer Support:** Employer completes employer section
6. **Insurer:** Claim lodged with insurer for assessment
7. **Ongoing Support:** Regular communication with employer regarding recovery

**Required Information:**
- Date and time of injury
- Detailed description of incident
- Nature of injury
- Medical evidence
- Wage details for benefits calculation

### 6.3 Return to Work

Company supports injured workers' return to work:

**Modified Duties:**
- Suitable duties identified for injured worker
- Gradual increase in hours/demands
- Based on medical advice and restrictions
- No penalty in pay during modified duties period

**Return to Work Plan:**
- Developed in consultation with injured worker, manager, and HR
- Regular review and adjustment
- Updated medical clearances
- Gradual transition to full duties

**Duration:** Modified duties typically 2-4 weeks, varies by injury

### 6.4 Support Services

- Employee Assistance Program (counselling)
- Rehabilitation services coordination
- Medical appointments during work hours
- Flexible arrangements during recovery
- Communication with insurer

## 7. Health and Hygiene

### 7.1 Office Environment Standards

Company maintains:

- Clean, sanitary workplace
- Adequate ventilation and lighting
- Temperature-controlled environments
- Functioning sanitation facilities
- Waste disposal systems
- Cleaning schedules

### 7.2 Illness and Contagion

**During Illness Outbreak (e.g., flu, COVID):**
- Employees feeling unwell should stay home
- Medical clearance required before returning
- Masks and hygiene supplies provided if needed
- Work-from-home alternatives available
- No penalty for absence due to illness

**Hand Hygiene:**
- Handwashing facilities available
- Sanitizer/handwashing stations provided
- Regular cleaning of high-touch surfaces

### 7.3 Mental Health and Wellbeing

- Mental health considered part of occupational health
- Psychological hazards identified and managed
- Stress management support provided
- Flexible arrangements for mental health treatment
- Confidential counselling via EAP
- Peer support networks available

## 8. Emergency Procedures

### 8.1 Emergency Response Plan

Company maintains emergency procedures for:

- Fire
- Medical emergency
- Severe weather
- Security threats
- Utility failures
- Other workplace emergencies

### 8.2 Fire Safety

**Fire Prevention:**
- Fire safety equipment (extinguishers, alarms) maintained
- Emergency exits clearly marked and accessible
- Exit paths kept clear
- Regular testing of alarms

**Fire Response:**
- Evacuation plan posted in work areas
- Assembly point identified (outside building)
- Muster roll used to account for all persons
- Emergency services (000) called by designated person
- No re-entry to building

**Drills:**
- Fire evacuation drills conducted annually
- All staff participate
- Procedures reviewed and updated

### 8.3 First Aid

- First aid station equipped with first aid supplies
- Trained first-aiders identified and available
- First aid procedures documented
- Serious injuries escalated to emergency services
- First aid kit maintained and restocked

### 8.4 Emergency Contact

- Emergency contact details recorded for all employees
- Emergency procedures communicated to all staff
- Evacuation procedures understood by all
- Assembly point and muster procedures

## 9. Substance Use Policy

### 9.1 Alcohol and Drug Prohibition

- Working under the influence of drugs/alcohol is prohibited
- Prohibited on company premises and during work
- Includes prescribed and non-prescribed substances affecting capacity

### 9.2 Testing and Screening

**Drug and Alcohol Testing:**
- Random testing may occur for safety-sensitive roles
- Pre-employment testing conducted
- Post-incident testing for serious incidents
- Tests conducted by authorized provider
- Results confidential

**Roles Subject to Testing:**
- Roles involving machinery or vehicles
- Roles with high safety risk
- Roles requiring full mental capacity
- Roles notified at hire

### 9.3 Support for Substance Misuse

Employees with substance misuse concerns:

- Encouraged to seek help proactively
- Confidential support via EAP
- Rehabilitation programs supported
- Leave and flexible arrangements considered
- Job protection if genuine recovery attempt

## 12. Non-Compliance and Penalties

### 12.1 Non-Compliance

Failure to comply with OHS policy may result in:

- Verbal warning
- Written warning
- Suspension or removal from role
- Disciplinary action up to termination
- Refusal to work in unsafe conditions (protected by law)

### 12.2 Serious Violations

Serious OHS violations:

- Willfully creating unsafe conditions
- Ignoring hazard warnings
- Not following emergency procedures
- Working impaired
- Failure to report incidents
- Retaliation against safety representatives

May result in immediate dismissal for serious misconduct.

### 12.3 Management Non-Compliance

Managers not providing safe workplace or supporting OHS:

- Subject to performance management
- Accountability for team safety
- Escalation to senior management
