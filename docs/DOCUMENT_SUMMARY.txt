KEY POLICY PROVISIONS - VICTORIA AUSTRALIA COMPLIANCE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

LEAVE ENTITLEMENTS
  • Annual Leave:        4 weeks/year (minimum per Fair Work Act)
  • Personal/Sick Leave: 10 days/year (exceeds minimum)
  • Long Service Leave:  8.67 weeks per 10 years (plus 17.5% loading)
  • Maternity Leave:     12 weeks PAID (exceeds 6-week statutory minimum)
  • Paternity Leave:     2 weeks PAID (exceeds 1-week statutory minimum)
  • Adoption Leave:      3 weeks PAID
  • Parental Leave:      Up to 12 weeks UNPAID + return to work support
  • Bereavement Leave:   3-5 days PAID
  • Carryover Limits:    Annual leave max 8 weeks (40 days)

REMUNERATION
  • Pay Frequency:       Fortnightly (26 pay periods per year)
  • Superannuation:      11.5% employer contribution (Industry Super)
  • Annual Review:       June each year (based on performance)
  • Salary Bands:        L1-L5 from $55,000-$240,000
  • Professional Dev:    $1,500-$3,000 per employee annually

FLEXIBLE WORKING
  • Right to Request:    Legislatively protected (Fair Work Act)
  • Options:             Flexible hours, compressed week, part-time, remote, job share
  • Trial Period:        Typically 4-8 weeks before confirmation
  • Performance:         No reduction in expectations

OCCUPATIONAL HEALTH & SAFETY
  • Commitment:          Safe workplace, hazard elimination, incident investigation
  • Worker Compensation: Covered by insurance, claims supported
  • Return to Work:      Modified duties, gradual return, HR support
  • Mental Health:       Mental health days supported, EAP available 24/7

PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
  • Annual Reviews:      June each year
  • Objective Setting:   3-5 SMART goals per employee
  • Rating Scale:        Exceeds / Meets / Developing / Below Expectation
  • Merit Increase:      0-4% typical range (based on performance rating)
  • Improvement Plans:   Support before formal action

CODE OF CONDUCT
  • Values:              Integrity, Excellence, Collaboration, Innovation, Respect, Accountability
  • Zero Tolerance:      Discrimination, harassment, bullying, sexual harassment
  • Confidentiality:     Client and company information protected
  • Compliance:          Fair Work Act, state and federal employment laws

EMPLOYEE SUPPORT SERVICES

  • Manager Support:     Regular feedback, development opportunities
  • Mentoring:           Internal and external development programs
  • Grievance Process:   Fair, transparent, documented resolution
