# Performance Management Policy

**Effective Date:** 1 July 2015  
**Last Updated:** 1 November 2026  
**Policy Review Date:** 1 November 2027  
**Document Reference:** HR-POL-008

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the framework for performance management, including goal-setting, feedback, development, and performance reviews for all employees of Pinnacle Solutions Pty Ltd. The policy supports employee development, accountability, and organizational success.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time, part-time, and fixed-term employees. Casual employees receive performance feedback but not formal annual reviews (unless in ongoing role).

---

## 3. Performance Management Philosophy

The company believes:

- **Continuous improvement:** Performance management is ongoing, not annual
- **Development focus:** Support employee growth and capability
- **Clarity:** Employees know what's expected
- **Fairness:** Consistent, transparent assessment
- **Support:** Managers support employee success
- **Accountability:** Performance matters for reward and development

---

## 4. Performance Framework

### 4.1 Performance Expectations

Every employee has clear expectations:

**What:**
- Role description and key responsibilities
- Core competencies required
- Behavioral standards
- Professional development expectations

**How:**
- Quality standards
- Timeliness and reliability
- Collaboration and teamwork
- Communication and responsiveness
- Adherence to policies

**Why:**
- Business objectives
- Client/customer needs
- Team goals
- Company values

### 4.2 Individual Performance Objectives

**Annual Objectives Setting:**

Each employee works with manager to set 3-5 objectives for the year:

**SMART Objectives:**
- **Specific:** Clear and detailed
- **Measurable:** Progress can be tracked
- **Achievable:** Realistic but challenging
- **Relevant:** Aligned with role and business
- **Time-bound:** Completion date specified

**Examples:**
- "Complete 5 client projects by 30 June, each within budget and timeline"
- "Mentor 2 junior team members in project management by December"
- "Achieve certification in [specific credential] by 30 September"
- "Improve customer satisfaction score from 78% to 85% by June"

**Objective Categories:**

| Category | Weight | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Business Results | 40-50% | Projects delivered, sales achieved, efficiency gains |
| Client/Customer | 20-30% | Satisfaction, retention, service quality |
| Team Contribution | 15-20% | Collaboration, mentoring, knowledge sharing |
| Personal Development | 10-15% | Skills gained, certifications, capability growth |

### 4.3 Competency Framework

Employees assessed on core competencies:

**Leadership (All levels):**
- Decision-making and judgment
- Vision and strategy
- Accountability and ownership
- Inspiration and influence

**Professional (All roles):**
- Technical expertise
- Quality of work
- Project/task management
- Problem-solving

**Interpersonal (All levels):**
- Communication
- Collaboration and teamwork
- Conflict resolution
- Relationship building

**Personal (All levels):**
- Initiative and drive
- Adaptability and resilience
- Learning and development
- Ethical conduct and integrity

## 5. Ongoing Performance Feedback

### 5.1 Regular Check-Ins

**Frequency:** Minimum monthly one-on-ones

**Purpose:**
- Progress against objectives
- Challenges and support needed
- Feedback on performance
- Development and growth opportunities

**Format:**
- Informal conversation, 30-45 minutes
- Two-way discussion
- Notes kept by manager
- Action items documented

### 5.2 Real-Time Feedback

Managers provide feedback:

- When performance is exceptional (positive reinforcement)
- When performance falls short (immediate correction)
- When behavior exemplifies company values
- When adjustment or improvement needed
- Constructively and respectfully

**Feedback Model:**
1. Describe specific behavior/situation
2. Explain impact (positive or negative)
3. Provide guidance or recognition
4. Plan forward action if needed

### 5.3 360-Degree Feedback (Senior Roles)

For management and senior roles:

**Process:**
- Feedback from manager, peers, team members, clients
- Anonymous feedback collected
- Summary report provided to employee
- Development conversation with HR and manager

**Timing:** Typically every 2 years

**Use:** Identifies development areas and strengths, informs promotions

## 6. Annual Performance Review

### 6.1 Review Schedule

**Timing:** June each year (aligned with financial year)

**Participants:**
- Employee
- Direct manager (primary reviewer)
- HR representative (for formal review)
- Executive/Senior manager (approval)

### 6.2 Review Process

**Step 1: Employee Self-Assessment (Due 1-2 weeks prior)**
- Employee completes self-assessment
- Reviews achievement against objectives
- Identifies strengths and development areas
- Proposes development goals for next year

**Step 2: Manager Assessment**
- Manager completes assessment
- Evaluates performance against objectives
- Assesses competencies demonstrated
- Identifies strengths and areas for development
- Considers 360 feedback if applicable

**Step 3: Performance Discussion Meeting**
- Manager and employee meet (1.5-2 hours)
- Review achievements and challenges
- Discuss performance rating
- Acknowledge strengths
- Identify development areas
- Agree on development actions
- Discuss career aspirations

**Step 4: Documentation**
- Formal performance review form completed
- Review outcomes documented
- Agreed actions and development plan recorded
- Signed by employee and manager
- Retained in personnel file

### 6.3 Performance Ratings

Employees rated on overall performance:

**Rating Scale:**

| Rating | Description | Frequency (Typical) | Salary Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exceeds Expectations (E) | Consistently delivers above requirements, demonstrates initiative | 15-20% | 3-4% increase |
| Meets Expectations (M) | Delivers against role requirements competently | 60-70% | 2-3% increase |
| Developing (D) | Meeting some expectations, development needed | 10-15% | 0-2% increase |
| Below Expectation (B) | Not meeting core expectations | 0-5% | 0% increase, performance plan |

**Consistency Check:**
- Distribution reviewed across department and company
- Consistency with peer performance
- Avoidance of rating inflation or deflation

### 6.4 Performance Rating Discussion

Ratings discussed in context of:

- Achievement of objectives (weight: 60%)
- Demonstration of competencies (weight: 30%)
- Behavior and values alignment (weight: 10%)
- External factors (market conditions, changes)
- Development and learning shown during year

## 7. Performance Improvement and Support

### 7.1 Developing Performance (Early Intervention)

If performance trending below expectations:

**Manager Actions:**
- Early conversation about concern
- Identify root causes (understanding, capability, environment)
- Offer support or resources needed
- Increase feedback frequency
- Provide coaching or training

**Outcomes:**
- Most issues resolved through support and clarity
- Performance improves and issue closes
- No formal action needed

### 7.2 Performance Improvement Plans (Formal)

**When Used:**
- Performance sustained below expectations (2+ months)
- Previous informal support unsuccessful
- Significant business impact
- Employee fails to reach developmental proficiency

**Plan Components:**

1. **Clear Performance Issue:**
   - Specific behaviors/outcomes not meeting expectation
   - Business impact identified
   - Compared to role requirements/benchmarks

2. **Improvement Targets:**
   - Specific, measurable standards to achieve
   - Timeline for improvement (typically 8-12 weeks)
   - Interim checkpoints

3. **Support Provided:**
   - Training or development activities
   - Mentoring or coaching
   - Resource allocation
   - Flexible arrangements (if needed)
   - Clear communication from manager

4. **Monitoring:**
   - Weekly or bi-weekly check-ins
   - Progress against targets tracked
   - Feedback on improvement
   - Documentation of progress

5. **Outcome:**
   - **Success:** Return to normal performance management
   - **Partial improvement:** Extension of plan possible
   - **No improvement:** May lead to disciplinary action up to termination

**Right to Support:**
- Employee aware of PIP process
- Opportunity to respond to concerns
- Support offered
- Right to representation/support person

## 8. Development and Career Planning

### 8.1 Learning and Development

The company supports:

- **Technical skills:** Industry-specific, technical training
- **Leadership development:** Management programs, coaching
- **Professional credentials:** Accountancy, engineering, IT qualifications
- **Personal development:** Communication, time management, resilience
- **Online learning:** LinkedIn Learning, platforms available

**Budget:** $1,500-$3,000 per employee annually (varies by level)

### 8.2 Career Development Discussions

**Frequency:** Annually, typically in performance review

**Discussion Topics:**
- Career interests and aspirations
- Skills and experience gained
- Internal role opportunities
- Development priorities
- Training needs
- Timeline for career progression
- Barriers or concerns

**Manager Role:**
- Mentor and guide
- Support realistic career planning
- Identify suitable opportunities
- Provide development opportunities
- Facilitate transitions

### 8.3 Succession Planning

For key roles:

- Potential successors identified
- Development needs assessed
- Development opportunities provided
- Cross-training arranged
- Knowledge transfer planned

## 9. Promotion and Advancement

### 9.1 Promotion Criteria

Promotions based on:

- **Performance:** Consistent strong performance in current role
- **Capability:** Demonstrated capability for higher-level role
- **Readiness:** Development areas addressed
- **Potential:** Ability to grow into wider responsibilities
- **Opportunity:** Role availability and business need
- **Fairness:** Merit-based, no favoritism

### 9.2 Promotion Process

**Opportunity Identified:**
- Internal advertising of position
- Internal candidates encouraged to apply
- External recruitment conducted if internal candidates insufficient

**Promotion Criteria:**
- Experience in similar role/level
- Performance track record
- Technical and interpersonal skills
- Readiness for expanded responsibilities
- Feedback from managers/colleagues

**Decision:**
- Candidates assessed through interview/panel
- Selection based on merit
- Offer made with new conditions (salary, title, start date)
- Unsuccessful candidate given feedback
- Development plan discussed if applicable

### 9.3 Promotion Adjustment Period

- New role has settling-in period (typically 3 months)
- Increased support and feedback
- Clear expectations for new role
- May extend or adjust if role change significant

## 10. Underperformance and Disciplinary Action

### 10.1 Performance Management vs. Discipline

**Performance Management:**
- Focus on improvement and support
- Addresses capability issues
- Developmental approach
- Improvement plans used

**Disciplinary Action:**
- Addresses behavioral/conduct issues
- Focus on compliance and accountability
- Formal process followed
- Sanctions applied

### 10.2 Conduct Issues Affecting Performance

Some performance issues have conduct/behavior component:

- Attendance/reliability issues (behavior)
- Attitude or commitment concerns (conduct)
- Disrespect toward others (behavior)
- Deliberate underperformance (conduct)

**Management:**
- May use performance management or disciplinary approach
- Depends on intent and circumstances
- Often combined approach
- Clear communication of issue and expectations

### 10.3 Termination for Performance

Termination for sustained poor performance possible after:

- Clear performance expectations communicated
- Support and resources provided
- Reasonable opportunity to improve (minimum 8-12 weeks)
- Formal improvement plan used
- Regular monitoring and feedback
- Fair process followed
- No improvement despite genuine effort

**Procedural Requirements:**
- Written notice of performance issue
- Opportunity to respond
- Support offered documented
- Regular review meetings held
- Clear communication on performance improvement
- Final warning given before termination
- Severance package (if applicable)

## 11. Performance Disputes

### 11.1 Dispute Process

If employee disputes performance rating:

1. **Raise with Manager:** Within 5 business days of review meeting
2. **Discussion:** Manager explains reasoning, employee's perspective heard
3. **If Unresolved:** Escalate to HR Manager within 5 days
4. **HR Review:** HR reviews assessment and documentation
5. **Decision:** HR Manager makes final decision on rating
6. **Communication:** Outcome communicated to employee
7. **Records:** Dispute and decision documented
